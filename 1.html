MYJOB - JCL 作业流 DSN 流向图

点击 STEP 节点查看详细信息；点击 DSN 圆点查看数据集信息并高亮链路；点击空白处恢复。

JCL 源码（示例）

//MYJOB    JOB (ACCT),'DSN FLOW DEMO',
//             CLASS=A,MSGCLASS=X
//STEP01   EXEC PGM=IEBGENER
//SYSUT1   DD DSN=INPUT.RAW.DATA,DISP=SHR
//SYSUT2   DD DSN=WORK.CLEAN.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP02   EXEC PGM=SORT
//SORTIN   DD DSN=WORK.CLEAN.DATA,DISP=SHR
//SORTOUT  DD DSN=WORK.SORTED.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP03   EXEC PGM=VALIDATE
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.SORTED.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=WORK.VALID.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP04   EXEC PGM=ENRICH
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.VALID.DATA,DISP=SHR
//REFDD    DD DSN=REF.CUSTOMER.MASTER,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=WORK.ENRICHED.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP05   EXEC PGM=AGGREGATE
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.ENRICHED.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=WORK.AGG.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP06   EXEC PGM=CALCRATE
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.AGG.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=WORK.RATE.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP07   EXEC PGM=FORMAT
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.RATE.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=REPORT.FINAL.TXT,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP08   EXEC PGM=ARCHIVE
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.ENRICHED.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=ARCHIVE.ENRICHED.DATA,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP09   EXEC PGM=AUDIT
//INDD     DD DSN=WORK.VALID.DATA,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=AUDIT.LOG.TXT,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
//STEP10   EXEC PGM=NOTIFY
//INDD     DD DSN=REPORT.FINAL.TXT,DISP=SHR
//OUTDD    DD DSN=NOTIFY.OUT.LOG,DISP=(NEW,CATLG)
